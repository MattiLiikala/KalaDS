# Writing

Interface text is part of the design. Keep it short, specific and in sentence case.

## Buttons

Start with a verb that says what happens: "Save changes", "Delete project", "Send invite".

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Say what the button does. The person knows what will happen before they press it.">
    <kds-stack direction="row" gap="sm">
      <kds-button variant="danger">Delete project</kds-button>
      <kds-button variant="ghost">Keep project</kds-button>
    </kds-stack>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="Don't use vague labels. 'Yes' and 'No' only make sense next to the question.">
    <kds-stack direction="row" gap="sm">
      <kds-button variant="danger">Yes</kds-button>
      <kds-button variant="ghost">No</kds-button>
    </kds-stack>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

## Form labels and helper text

- Labels are nouns: "Email", "Billing address". Don't end them with a colon.
- Use helper text for format or limits ("At least 12 characters"), not to repeat the label.
- Don't put essential information in placeholders: they disappear when people type.

## Error messages

Say what went wrong and how to fix it, in the person's terms.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Name the problem and the fix.">
    <kds-input label="Email" value="anna@" error="Enter a full email address, like name@example.com."></kds-input>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="Don't blame, shout or use codes. 'Invalid input' doesn't say what to change.">
    <kds-input label="Email" value="anna@" error="ERROR: Invalid input."></kds-input>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>

## Notifications

- The heading says what happened, in a few words: "Payment failed", "Changes saved".
- The message adds what to do next, if anything.
- Use `danger` and `warning` only when people need to act: those are announced immediately by screen
  readers.

## Links

Link text says where the link goes: "Read the billing guide", not "click here" or "more".

## Style

- Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headings: "Create account", not "Create Account".
- Use numerals for numbers: "3 files", not "three files".
- Leave out "please" and "successfully": "Changes saved", not "Your changes were successfully saved".
