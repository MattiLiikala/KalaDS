## Usage

- Pick the variant by what people need to do: `info` for news, `success` to confirm, `warning` when
  something needs attention soon, `danger` when something failed or is blocked.
- The `heading` says what happened in a few words; the message says what to do next.
- Use `danger` and `warning` sparingly: they are announced immediately and interrupt screen reader users.
- Make a notification `dismissible` unless people must act on it first.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="A short heading that says what happened, and a message with the next step.">
    <kds-notification variant="danger" heading="Payment failed">
      Your card was declined. Update your billing details to keep your plan.
    </kds-notification>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="Don't use danger for good news or routine information: the colour and announcement don't match.">
    <kds-notification variant="danger" heading="Welcome back!">
      You have 3 new messages.
    </kds-notification>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
