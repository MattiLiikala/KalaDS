## Usage

- Use a toggle for a setting that takes effect immediately, like turning notifications on.
- In a form that's submitted later, use a checkbox instead.
- The label names the setting, not its state: "Show notifications", not "On".

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="The label names the setting; the toggle shows its state.">
    <kds-toggle checked>Show notifications</kds-toggle>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="Don't label the state: 'On' doesn't say what is on.">
    <kds-toggle checked>On</kds-toggle>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
