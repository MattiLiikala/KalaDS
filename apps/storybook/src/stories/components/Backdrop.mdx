## Usage

- Use a backdrop to dim the page while something temporary covers it, such as a loading message.
- It doesn't trap focus. For anything people interact with, use `kds-dialog` or `kds-drawer`, which have
  their own backdrop.
- Keep it short-lived, and always remove it when the work finishes or fails.
