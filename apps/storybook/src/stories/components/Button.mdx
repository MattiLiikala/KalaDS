## Usage

- Use **one primary button** per view or section, for the main action. Use `secondary` for other
  important actions and `ghost` for the rest (cancel, back, less important choices).
- Use `danger` only for actions that destroy something, and say what will be destroyed: "Delete project".
- Use a button for actions. To go somewhere else, use `kds-link`.
- Solid buttons cut their label out of the fill: check **Guidelines/Knockout and Surfaces** before placing
  one on anything other than a light surface.
