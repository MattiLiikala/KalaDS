## Usage

- Use a badge for a short status or count next to something: "Online", "Beta", "3 new".
- Keep it to one or two words. Badges aren't interactive: for filters or removable tags use `kds-chip`.
- Use badges on light surfaces only.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="A short status next to what it describes.">
    <kds-stack direction="row" gap="sm" align="center">
      <kds-typography>Build</kds-typography>
      <kds-badge variant="success">Passed</kds-badge>
    </kds-stack>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="Don't put sentences in a badge: it's for a word or two.">
    <kds-badge variant="warning">Your storage is almost full, consider upgrading</kds-badge>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
