## Usage

- Use tabs to switch between views of the same thing, such as a project's overview, activity and settings.
- Keep labels to one or two words. Each `kds-tab` needs a `kds-tab-panel` with the same `value`.
- Don't use tabs for steps people must take in order.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Views of one project, with short labels.">
    <kds-tabs label="Project" value="overview">
      <kds-tab value="overview">Overview</kds-tab>
      <kds-tab value="activity">Activity</kds-tab>
      <kds-tab-panel value="overview">A summary of the project.</kds-tab-panel>
      <kds-tab-panel value="activity">Recent changes.</kds-tab-panel>
    </kds-tabs>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="Don't use tabs for a sequence: people can skip ahead and miss steps.">
    <kds-tabs label="Checkout" value="details">
      <kds-tab value="details">1. Details</kds-tab>
      <kds-tab value="payment">2. Payment</kds-tab>
      <kds-tab value="confirm">3. Confirm</kds-tab>
      <kds-tab-panel value="details">Your details</kds-tab-panel>
      <kds-tab-panel value="payment">Payment</kds-tab-panel>
      <kds-tab-panel value="confirm">Confirm</kds-tab-panel>
    </kds-tabs>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
