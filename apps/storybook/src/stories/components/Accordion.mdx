## Usage

- Use an accordion for long, optional detail that people scan by heading: FAQs, advanced settings.
- Keep headings short and specific, so people can find the section they need.
- By default one section is open at a time; add `multiple` when people compare sections.
- Don't hide content that everyone needs.

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="Optional detail behind clear headings.">
    <kds-accordion>
      <kds-accordion-item heading="Can I change my plan later?">Yes, at any time from Billing.</kds-accordion-item>
      <kds-accordion-item heading="How do refunds work?">Refunds reach your card within 10 days.</kds-accordion-item>
    </kds-accordion>
  </Do>
  <Dont caption="Don't collapse the one thing everyone needs to read.">
    <kds-accordion>

    </kds-accordion>
  </Dont>
</DoDont>
