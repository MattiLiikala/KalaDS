# Shadow Tokens

KalaDS has two shadow families, each with three levels.

- **Crisp** shadows are offset diagonally with no blur, in `neutral-300`. They are for surfaces that sit
  in the page: **card**, **paper** and **notification**, chosen with their `elevation` attribute.
- **Soft** shadows are blurred, in semi-transparent `neutral-900` (`color.neutral-alpha.*`). They are for
  everything that floats above the page: **menus**, **autocomplete popups** and **dialogs**.

Offsets and blurs use the `px` size values, so the edges stay sharp at any font size.

The offsets are also available on their own as `--kds-semantic-shadow-crisp-offset-1` to `-3`: an
interactive card moves by this distance on hover, so it presses down into its shadow.
