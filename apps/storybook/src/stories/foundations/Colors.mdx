# Colors

Colour tokens come in two layers. **Palettes** (`color.*`) are the raw scales. **Semantic tokens**
(`semantic.*`) give them a job, such as a page background, body text or a danger border. Use semantic
tokens in product code, and palettes only for data visualisation.

Each swatch shows what it can carry on the default page background: **Text** passes 4.5:1 for body text,
**UI** passes 3:1 for borders, icons and large text. Swatches without a label are for backgrounds and
fills only.

## Status

In every status palette, steps 600 and up pass 4.5:1 for text. Components use the 700 step (the semantic
`*-strong` tokens) for status text and icons, and 100–200 for tinted backgrounds.
