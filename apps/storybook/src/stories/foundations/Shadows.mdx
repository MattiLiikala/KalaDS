# Shadows

Two shadow families, three levels each.

- **Crisp** shadows are offset diagonally with no blur, in `neutral-300`. They are for surfaces that sit
  in the page: **card**, **paper** and **notification**, chosen with their `elevation` attribute.
  Notifications use the crisp shadow in their own status colour.
- **Soft** shadows are blurred, in semi-transparent `neutral-900`. They are for things that float above
  the page: **menus**, **autocomplete popups** and **dialogs**.

Offsets and blurs use the px size values, so edges stay sharp at any font size.
