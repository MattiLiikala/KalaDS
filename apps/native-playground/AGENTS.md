the KalaDS skill for the platform you're working on and follow its rules:

- `.agents/skills/kalads-react-native/SKILL.md` (@kalads/react-native-components 0.0.0)

```sh
node .agents/skills/kalads-react-native/scripts/check.mjs <changed files>
```
